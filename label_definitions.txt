# One definition per line. These are also the baseline model's prompt.

scene_identification: asks to identify a film or television work from a remembered scene, image, line, or sequence
production_detail: primarily asks how a film or television work was made, performed, edited, credited, translated, or technically achieved in the real world
plot_interpretation: primarily asks why an in-story event happens or what a character, ending, title, or theme means inside the fictional work
fact_context: primarily asks for a checkable external fact or relationship about a work, release, language, or technology, excluding production technique and in-story interpretation
